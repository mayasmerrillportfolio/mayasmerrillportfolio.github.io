No Vacancy
Project

Portland, Oregon

While working as a communications and media intern for Field States Urban design firm in Portland, I managed the field.states and novacancy social media pages on LinkedIn and Instagram. "The No Vacancy Project is all about filling empty storefronts with vibrant businesses and their creative energy – to benefit local enterprise and bring new vitality to our city's streets. The project provides access to resources, technical support, and community." Visit novacancyproject.com to learn more, and visit @novacancyproject on Instagram or click on the posts below.

While promoting No Vacancy to the public, I took photos, videos and interviewed stakeholders to capture how the initiative has impacted their daily lives and the communities in Old Town in Portland, Oregon.
