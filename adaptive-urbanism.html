Adaptive Urbanism
at Field States

Portland, Oregon

While working as a communications intern for Field States, a Portland-based urban design firm, I filmed and edited a video introducing their original framework called 'Adaptive Urbanism.' Adaptive Urbanism "treats cities as learning systems, capable of adapting to succeed in an ever-changing and unpredictable landscape. Our focused engagements are designed around the complex challenges we observe in cities, and our discrete deliverables are tailored to meet the specific needs of each client." Using scenes of Portland, clips from previous projects and animated branding assets, this video helps introduce the framework to broader audiences, stakeholders and potential collaborators.
